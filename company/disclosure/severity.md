# Severity Scale

We assess severity using CVSS v3.1 as a baseline, adjusted for business
context. Remediation targets are guided by the resulting severity.

| Severity | CVSS range | Remediation target |
|----------|-----------|--------------------|
| Critical | 9.0 - 10.0 | 7 days |
| High | 7.0 - 8.9 | 30 days |
| Medium | 4.0 - 6.9 | 90 days |
| Low | 0.1 - 3.9 | Best effort |

> Severity is a starting point, not a guarantee. Exploitability, data
> sensitivity, and blast radius may raise or lower the final rating.
