# Scope

The following assets are **in scope** for this program:

- Production web applications operated by {ORG_NAME}
- Public APIs documented at the official developer portal
- Official mobile applications published under the {ORG_NAME} vendor account

The following are explicitly **out of scope**:

- Third-party services and infrastructure not operated by {ORG_NAME}
- Denial-of-service (DoS) and volumetric attacks
- Social engineering of staff, contractors, or customers
- Physical attacks against offices or data centers

If you are unsure whether a target is in scope, contact us before testing.
