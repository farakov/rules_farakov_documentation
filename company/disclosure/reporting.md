# Reporting Process

To report a vulnerability:

1. Email **{SECURITY_CONTACT}** with a clear, descriptive subject line.
2. Include a detailed description, affected asset, and reproduction steps.
3. Attach any proof-of-concept code, requests, or screenshots.
4. Provide your preferred contact method for follow-up.

Encrypt sensitive reports using our PGP key, available at the security page.

## What to expect

| Stage | Target time |
|-------|-------------|
| Acknowledgement of report | Within 3 business days |
| Initial triage and severity assignment | Within 10 business days |
| Status update cadence | Every 14 days until resolution |
| Resolution target | Based on severity (see Severity Scale) |
