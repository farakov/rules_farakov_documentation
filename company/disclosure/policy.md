# Responsible Disclosure Policy

{ORG_NAME} values the work of security researchers and is committed to working
with the community to verify, reproduce, and respond to legitimate reported
vulnerabilities. This document describes how to report a vulnerability, what
you can expect from us, and the commitments we make in return.

We ask that you give us a reasonable opportunity to remediate an issue before
any public disclosure, and that your research avoids privacy violations,
service degradation, and destruction of data.
